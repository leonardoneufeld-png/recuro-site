Privacy

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. The calculator runs in your browser; the numbers you type are not stored or sent.

If you switch the language, that choice is kept in your browser’s local storage so the page opens in it next time. It never leaves your device.

Booking a call takes you to Cal.com. Cal.com processes what you enter there (name, email, time) under its own privacy policy. Recuro uses it only to hold the call.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

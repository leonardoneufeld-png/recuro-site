01

Till and sales

Today
The till records every sale. The report is printed at closing and filed.
What Recuro builds
The till on the counter stays. Recuro reads it: what sold, at what hour, on which day.
Where it goes
Every sale lowers the stock count and shows ordering what next week will need.

02

Stock

Today
Counted by hand with a clipboard after closing, once a week if the week allows.
What Recuro builds
A running count. Sales take stock out, deliveries put it back, and a short spot check corrects the difference.
Where it goes
When an item runs low, ordering already knows.

03

Ordering and suppliers

Today
Orders go out by phone and WhatsApp, from memory, usually the evening before.
What Recuro builds
The system drafts each order from stock and sales. A person checks it before it goes to the supplier.
Where it goes
The sent order waits for its delivery note and its invoice.

04 Running at a client

Staff hours and payroll

Today
Timesheets on paper, shift swaps in a group chat, and an Excel file at month end.
What Recuro builds
Staff log hours, swap shifts, report sick and request days off in a chat. The monthly statement is ready at month end.
Where it goes
Hours reach payroll already checked. Once the till is connected, staff cost sits next to sales for each day.

05

Cost of goods

Today
Calculated once, when the menu was written. Supplier prices have changed since.
What Recuro builds
What each product costs to make, recalculated when a supplier invoice shows a new price.
Where it goes
Margin per product, next to what sold.

06

Invoices

Today
Supplier invoices pile up in a folder until someone types them in.
What Recuro builds
Incoming invoices are read and matched to the order and the delivery. Outgoing ones are drawn up from what was delivered, and a person checks them before they go out.
Where it goes
New prices go to cost of goods. Checked invoices are ready for the bookkeeper.

One platform

The routine runs by itself.

A sale lowers the stock. Low stock drafts an order. The invoice is matched to that order, hours reach payroll, and the cost of each product stays current. A person approves before anything is sent. The rest of the day goes to guests and sales.

How it works

The AI chooses. The code does the work. A person confirms.

The language model only makes suggestions. Ordinary code does the sums and keeps the records. Nothing changes until someone on your team says yes.

What Recuro builds

Each piece starts small and fits the way your team already works.

  1. Staff hours and shift planning Running at a client

    Staff log hours, swap shifts, report sick and request days off in a chat. The monthly statement is ready at month end.

  2. Semi-automatic ordering

    The system drafts the order from stock and sales. A person checks it before it goes to the supplier.

  3. Automatic stock counts

    Stock levels stay current without a clipboard at closing time.

  4. Cost of goods

    What each product really costs, updated when supplier prices change.

  5. Invoices

    Generated from what was delivered or sold, ready to check and send.

It starts with one café. The same setup works across several locations.

How it works

The AI chooses. The code does the work. A person confirms.

The language model only makes suggestions. Ordinary code does the sums and keeps the records. Nothing changes until someone on your team says yes.
